📚 Interactive Science Simulations

Browser-based activities for lower-secondary science. Nothing to install — pick one and go.

🧪 Chemistry

⚗️ Chemical Formula Challenge latest
Write the formula for an ionic compound from its name or its ion pair. Two difficulty levels, a countdown timer, hints, streak tracking and audio feedback.
🔷 IonQuest
A grid puzzle — find adjacent ions that combine into the named compound, with the charges balancing correctly.
🥊 Reactivity Rumble latest
Reactions of metals with water, steam and dilute acid, plus displacement — pick the correctly balanced equation, with state symbols, and match the vigour vocabulary.

🔬 Biology

🧬 Specimen Bench
Free-recall revision across the N-Level Biology syllabus — type the answer yourself rather than choosing from options, closer to what the actual paper asks.
